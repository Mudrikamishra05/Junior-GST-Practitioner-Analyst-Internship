# GST Practitioner Analyst Internship Portfolio

## Overview

This repository contains my work completed during the GST Practitioner Analyst Internship.

The portfolio covers GST compliance, taxation procedures, reporting frameworks, refund management, internal audits, risk assessment, policy analysis, and strategic improvements.

The objective of this portfolio is to demonstrate my understanding of GST processes, business analysis, regulatory research, documentation, and compliance management.

---

## Internship Projects

### Week 1 – GST Registration and Compliance Planning

**Topics Covered:**
- GST Registration Framework
- Compliance Planning
- Documentation Requirements
- Process Mapping
- Compliance Calendar

**Key Learning:** Understanding the importance of GST registration, documentation, and structured compliance planning.

### Week 2 – GST Computation and Reporting

**Topics Covered:**
- GST Components: CGST, SGST, IGST and UTGST
- GST Tax Computation
- Input Tax Credit (ITC)
- GST Return Filing
- GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B
- Error Reduction Practices

**Key Learning:** Understanding GST calculations, ITC verification, tax reporting, and return filing procedures.

### Week 3 – GST Refund Process Analysis

**Topics Covered:**
- Refund Eligibility Assessment
- Documentation Requirements
- Refund Application Procedures
- Refund Processing Timelines
- Common Challenges
- Process Improvement Recommendations

**Key Learning:** Understanding different GST refund categories, supporting documents, application procedures, and the importance of accurate documentation.

### Week 4 – GST Compliance Audit Framework

**Topics Covered:**
- Internal Audit Planning
- GST Risk Assessment
- Internal Control Mechanisms
- Compliance Checklists
- Audit Procedures
- Risk Mitigation Strategies
- Reconciliation Processes

**Key Learning:** Understanding how internal audits identify compliance gaps, evaluate risks, and strengthen GST control systems.

### Week 5 – GST Policy Change Analysis

**Topics Covered:**
- GST Policy Developments
- GST Rate Rationalisation
- Business Impact Assessment
- Sector-Wise Analysis
- Regulatory Changes
- Strategic Recommendations
- Future Compliance Trends

**Key Learning:** Analysing how GST policy developments influence business operations, financial decisions, and compliance practices.

### Week 6 – GST Capstone Report

**Topics Covered:**
- Comprehensive Review of GST Compliance Systems
- Strengths and Weaknesses Assessment
- SWOT Analysis
- Emerging Trends in GST
- Artificial Intelligence and Automation
- Future Outlook
- Strategic Framework Development
- Practitioner Perspective

**Key Learning:** Integrating previous learning to evaluate existing GST practices and recommend practical improvements for future compliance management.
